# Idea Refine

1. Reframe the decision and identify audience, outcome, constraints, and success measures.
2. Generate only enough distinct options to expose meaningful trade-offs.
3. Compare value, feasibility, risk, and differentiation; identify assumptions to validate.
4. Recommend a direction and record MVP scope and explicit non-goals when a durable artifact is useful.

## Verification checklist

- [ ] The recommendation follows from stated criteria.
- [ ] Material assumptions and non-goals are visible.
